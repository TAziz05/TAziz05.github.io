Data science & more

Where it started.

US Car Accidents Analysis

My first data science project, built with Team Gravity Wells at AI Camp in summer 2022. We used Kaggle data on US traffic accidents from 2016 to 2021 to predict how severe a crash would be from conditions like weather, temperature and time of day. We compared KNN, SVC, random forest and neural network models, and random forest won at about 68% accuracy.
